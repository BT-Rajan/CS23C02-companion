CS23C02 Companion

The app that comes with your CS23C02 textbook. Read the lessons offline, step through every program one line at a time, ask the tutor to show you a program or guide you while you type it, practise, and check your own code.

You need an Android phone (Android 8 or newer) and the activation code printed inside your copy of the book.

How to start

  1. Install the app and create an account with your email address.
  2. Enter the code we email you to confirm the address.
  3. Scratch the panel in your book and enter the activation code.
