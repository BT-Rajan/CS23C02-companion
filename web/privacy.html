Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password hash (Argon2id; we never store the password itself).
  • Activation: which edition of the book your account has unlocked. Activation codes are stored only as keyed hashes.
  • Learning records: lessons you finish, your answers to practice questions, programs you submit for checking and their results, and unit assessment attempts and scores.
  • Security records: sign-in events and a keyed hash of the network address they came from, used to stop abuse.

What we do not collect

No advertising, no analytics or tracking libraries, no location, no contacts. Programs you run on your phone stay on your phone; only programs you choose to Check are sent to our server, where they run in an isolated sandbox and are kept with the result.

Why

To let you sign in, unlock the book's content, keep your progress across phones, check your programs and score assessments.

Email

We email you only to confirm your address and to reset your password.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your records while your account exists. To delete your account and its records, write to the support address on the home page from the email address of the account.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. It is stored on servers we rent; the hosting company has no right to use it.
